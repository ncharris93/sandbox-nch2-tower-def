# Headless harness compares strategies over ten waves

## Outcome
A script runs scripted layouts (spam, long weak maze, switchback kill zone, single-type builds) through all ten waves in the real sim and prints lives lost and money per wave per strategy.

## Why now
Without measurement, tuning is guesswork; the report is the job's deliverable and grows from slice one.
