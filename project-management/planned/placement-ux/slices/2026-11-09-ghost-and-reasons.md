# Placement ghost shows range and why a tile is refused

## Outcome
In build mode a 2×2 ghost with the tower's range follows the cursor, tinted valid/invalid; hovering an invalid spot shows its exact reason (funds, blocked, trap, anti-juggling). Unaffordable tower types look disabled in the build menu.

## Why now
The biggest gap between 'works' and 'understandable'; every later UX piece sits on the hover state.
