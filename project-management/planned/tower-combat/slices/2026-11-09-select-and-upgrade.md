# Select a tower, read its stats, upgrade to Level 3

## Outcome
Clicking a placed tower highlights it, shows its range and a panel with type, level, damage, rate, range, splash/slow, upgrade cost and sell value. Upgrade buttons take each tower Level 0→3 with identity-reinforcing steps (Cannon: damage → splash/range → major both; Rapid: rate → damage/range → high sustained; Frost: slow → duration/range → splash slow) and visible upgrade feedback.

## Why now
Upgrades are the economy's main decision; needs all three towers to exist first.
