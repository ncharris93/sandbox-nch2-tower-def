# Rapid tower kills enemies

## Outcome
A tower-type selector offers Rapid. Enemies have health and health bars. A Rapid tower fires frequent visible projectiles at the enemy closest to the exit; enemies lose health, die at zero with a small death effect, and award a placeholder bounty.

## Why now
Proves the whole combat pipeline (targeting → projectile → damage → death) on the simplest tower; everything else plugs into it.
