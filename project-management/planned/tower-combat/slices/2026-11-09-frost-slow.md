# Frost slows enemies visibly

## Outcome
Frost is buildable: low damage, distinct projectile, applies a partial non-stacking slow; slowed enemies are tinted and visibly slower; Frost prefers unslowed furthest-along targets.

## Why now
Completes the synergy triangle; touches enemy movement speed, so it lands before upgrades multiply the stats.
