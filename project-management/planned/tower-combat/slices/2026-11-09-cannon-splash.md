# Cannon splash punishes clusters

## Outcome
Cannon is buildable: slow, heavy shells aimed at the enemy whose splash catches the most neighbors; a visible explosion damages every enemy in the radius.

## Why now
Area damage and cluster targeting are the trickiest targeting rule and the core reason to maze into kill zones.
