# Removing a tower re-routes enemies onto the shorter path

## Outcome
When a tower leaves the board mid-wave, active enemies take the newly opened route without teleporting. Demonstrated through the sim's remove-tower operation (and Sell once tower-combat lands it).

## Why now
Repath must hold for both directions of layout change before anti-juggling restricts one of them.
