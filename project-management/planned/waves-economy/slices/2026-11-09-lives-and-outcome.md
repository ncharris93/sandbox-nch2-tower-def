# Leaks cost lives; win, lose and restart

## Outcome
Enemies reaching the exit visibly enter it, vanish and cost lives (Boss more) with a leak cue. Lives at 0 shows a defeat overlay; clearing wave 10 shows victory. Both offer Restart, which yields a clean new game.

## Why now
Turns the sandbox into a game with stakes; restart cleanliness is a PROMPT verification item.
