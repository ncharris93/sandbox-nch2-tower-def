# Ten-wave flow with intermissions

## Outcome
A wave schedule of ten waves spawns enemies over time with per-wave cadence. Between waves the game sits in intermission until the player presses 'Start Next Wave'. The HUD shows current wave and enemies remaining, with a clear wave-start/wave-cleared transition.

## Why now
The game loop's backbone; archetypes and outcomes all plug into it.
