# Six enemy archetypes that play differently

## Outcome
Normal, Runner, Swarm, Brute, Regenerator and Boss each have distinct visuals and behavior (speed, health, regen, count/spacing, life cost, partial slow resistance for Boss), and waves 1–10 use them per PROMPT's teaching curve.

## Why now
Archetype variety is what makes each tower type matter; needs the wave flow to place them.
